Why the models price differently

Both models share the same diffusive drift μ and volatility σ (set in the control panel). Merton adds a Poisson jump component on top, which fattens the tail — usually to the downside for negative jump means. That extra tail risk is exactly what an option buyer pays up for, so the Merton price sits above the Black-Scholes/GBM price.
